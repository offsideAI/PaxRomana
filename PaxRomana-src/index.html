PAX ROMANA

Rome never fell. Twelve centuries on, her legions still garrison the châteaux of Gaul.
From the ridge above the valley, one rifle will change that.

Mission

Break into Château de Montvallon, find the key to the keep vault, and carry the Crown Jewels out through the gate in your loot bag.

Thin out the garrison from the ridge first. Sentries who see you, missed shots and fallen comrades raise the alarm.
